GAME RULES

1. Guess the Mystery Word

Both player holds a secret word on their forehead while their teammate asks yes-or-no questions to figure it out as fast as possible.

2. Three Allowed Answers

The clue-giver can only reply with OO (Yes), HINDI (No), or PWEDE (Maybe). Giving extra hints or gestures is forbidden!

3. Penalties & Fastest Win

Breaking the rule adds a +2 second penalty to your clock. Once both teams guess their word, the team with the fastest time wins!
