Memes, Virality, and Influencers

In today’s digital world, content spreads faster than ever. Platforms like Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, and Reddit allow ideas, trends, and personalities to reach millions within hours.

Memes and Online Humor

A meme is an idea, joke, image, video, or phrase that spreads quickly online. Memes are humorous, relatable, simple to understand and share, and adaptable, allowing users to remix them for different contexts.

DIFFERENT TYPES OF MEMES

Social Memes

It focuses on everyday life, relationships, school, work, and human behavior. They help people laugh at their shared challenges and remind them that others go through the same things.

Political Memes

It is created to express opinions, reactions, or criticism about government leaders, laws, policies, and current issues. They often use humor, irony, or satire to make serious topics more engaging and easier to understand. These memes can spread political awareness, spark discussions, or even influence public opinion.

Cultural Memes

It is based on popular culture such as movies, music, TV shows, fashion, internet trends, and famous personalities. They reflect what’s popular in society at a certain time. These memes spread fast because they are based on shared cultural experiences that people already recognize.

FILIPINO MEMES

Filipino Memes

Filipino memes usually come from movie lines, TV show moments, and viral videos that capture the expressive and playful nature of Filipinos

“Bakit parang kasalanan ko?”

comes from the movie , Four Sisters and a Wedding (2013), where Bea Alonzo as Bobbie Salazar delivers the line with emotional intensity. The meme went viral around 2017 and continues to be used humorously for unjust accusations or shifting blame.

“Keratin?!”

meme originates from Maja Salvador’s 2024 Keratin Plus commercial, where she humorously repeats the word “Keratin” in a high-pitched tone. This meme is used to react to unexpected or fancy-sounding English words.

“What hafen, Vella?”

meme comes from a 2013 performance by Christopher Diwata, who impersonated Taylor Lautner on the “Kalokalike” segment of It’s Showtime. The video clip began circulating on TikTok in late 2024, but it wasn’t until Netflix Philippines shared a meme of it on Facebook that it broke into mainstream consciousness. Taylor Lautner himself acknowledged it in a TikTok video. This meme is used humorously for dramatic or emotional situations.

“Jusko po, Jusko po!”

meme originates from Arman Salon, a Filipino content creator known for his comedic and exaggerated skits. This meme is used to humorously react to unexpected or overwhelming events.



VIRALITY



Virality

Content goes viral when it spreads rapidly, often driven by humor, relatability, shock, or emotion. Filipino netizens’ active sharing plays a major role. Viral posts start with content creation, gain early engagement from friends or followers, are amplified by algorithms, and reach a wider audience through mass sharing. Filipino influencers gained fame through viral videos that showcase netizens’ humor and creativity



FILIPINO INFLUENCERS

Cong TV (Lincoln Velasquez)

launched his YouTube channel in 2008 and became one of the country’s most recognized content creators. He is also the vocalist of the band COLN and the founder of Team Payaman. In 2022, he received several awards, including Gaming Male Ambassador of the Year, YouTube Partner of the Year (Male), Instagram Creator of the Year (Male), and the Above and Beyond Award from Tier One Entertainment’s IRL Awards. Cong is widely known for his signature phrases, “What’s up sa inyo, mga paa?” and “Paawer!

Niana Guerrero and Ranz Kyle

Are sibling content creators famous for their energetic dance covers and family vlogs. They won Nickelodeon’s “Favorite Pinoy Internet Star” award in 2019 and the “Entertainment Champion Award” from the Creator and Influencer Council of the Philippines (CICP) in 2020. Niana was also noticed by K-pop idols such as EXO’s Chanyeol, BTS’s J-Hope, GOT7’s BamBam, among others.

Mimiyuuuh (Jeremy Sancebuche)

Rose to fame in 2019 through the viral video challenge of “Dalagang Pilipina” by ALLMO$T. That same year, she received the Trending of the Year award from E! Philippines for her impact on online content. Known as “The People’s Ghorl,” Mimiyuuuh became famous for her signature intro “It’s your ghurl, Mimiyuuuh, oh yeah!” Her iconic pink plastic cabinet also became a recognizable backdrop in many of her early videos.

Sassa Gurl (Felix Petate)

Became popular in 2020 during the pandemic for her funny TikTok skits. She later appeared in Drag Den (2022) and made history as the first LGBTQIA+ calendar model for White Castle Whisky in 2022. In 2024, she starred in the political thriller film Balota. Known as the “Pambansang Mima,” Sassa Gurl is admired for her versatile fashion sense and bold style choices

Esnyr (Esnyr John Ranollo)

Gained attention for his relatable TikTok skits that brought back high school nostalgia. He later appeared in films such as Balota (2024). Esnyr has stepped up his game by creating a YouTube skit series titled HAYSKUL CRUSH MOMENTS, featuring a diverse cast of characters including Andrei, Precious, Bogart, Ma’am Castro, and more. His characters became so popular that they were even featured in a weekly task during his stint on Pinoy Big Brother: Celebrity Collab Edition (2025).

Erwan Heussaff

A Filipino-French content creator, chef, and entrepreneur known for showcasing Filipino cuisine and culture through his digital video channel named FEATR. His documentary-style videos highlight local food stories and traditions. He won the Social Media Category of the James Beard Broadcast Media Awards for his Instagram account.

INFLUENCERS



Influencers

In today’s digital age, influencers shape Filipino pop culture by entertaining and reflecting values, humor, and lifestyles. Their content bridges mainstream media and everyday online life:



FILIPINO INFLUENCERS

Heart Evangelista (Love Marie Ongpauco-Escudero)

Combines celebrity status with digital influence, using her platform to showcase fashion, art, and lifestyle. She is known for hand-painting luxury bags that reflect her creativity. As a global fashion icon, she attends New York Fashion Week and collaborates with international brands.

Small Laude (Marissa Eduardo-Laude)

Gives viewers a fun and authentic look into the life of the Filipino elite. She is known for her iconic line, “Of course! No bashing, it’s for fun!” which reflects her lighthearted and positive personality. Beyond entertainment, she also uses her platform to contribute to society through outreach programs, earning her the Volvo PH Ironmark Award in 2023.

Arshie Larga (Ramon Christian Larga)

A licensed pharmacist and trusted health advocate on TikTok. He creates informative content on safe medicine use and helps raise funds for those who cannot afford their medications. In 2024, he was included in Forbes’ “30 Under 30 Asia” list for the Media, Marketing, and Advertising Category, which recognizes outstanding young entrepreneurs, leaders, and influencers under the age of 30 from the Asia-Pacific region.

Coach Lyqa (Lyqa Maravilla)

A licensed psychometrician, professional teacher, and motivational speaker known for her educational content on grammar, productivity, and life skills. Through her #TeamLyqa community, she promotes accessible learning and test preparation. In 2017, she was recognized as one of YouTube’s NextUp winners.

Alodia Gosiengfiao (Alodia Almira Gosiengfiao-Quimbo)

A Filipino-Chinese content creator and a pioneer in cosplay and gaming. Her success helped bring these once niche interests into the mainstream culture and inspired a new generation of digital artists and gamers.



CANCEL CULTURE IN NETIZENSHIP



Cancel Culture

It refers to the cultural phenomenon of publicly criticizing, boycotting, or withdrawing support from individuals, groups, or brands for perceived offensive actions or statements. It often spreads rapidly on social media, where public shaming and polarized opinions can cause emotional distress, content removal, or serious harm to someone’s reputation and career.

Netizenship

Being a responsible and ethical citizen of the internet. It involves using digital platforms carefully, respecting others, and contributing positively to online communities.

REMEMBER!

When engaging online, you must think before you post or react. Be respectful and responsible, protect your peace, and avoid joining trends without verifying the facts. Encourage learning and reflection instead of rejection when others make mistakes
